Why this mix

Choosing the top five by volume gives four southern states plus Maharashtra, so results would not transfer. The portfolio spans volume leaders (TN, AP), conversion leaders (KA, OD at 72%), a large northern state (UP) and two catch-up states including the North-East (Bihar, Assam). Each pilot is paired with a matched comparison district.
